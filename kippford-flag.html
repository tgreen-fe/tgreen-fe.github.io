The village flag of Kippford

Kippford is a sailing village on the Solway coast. I entered the community's village flag competition, and my design was adopted.

Context
Community flag competition, Kippford, Scotland
Role
Sole designer
Period
2022 to 2023
Status
Adopted, in use

The brief

A flag has to read at a distance, in motion and from both sides, so it needs a few flat colours and simple geometry. For Kippford it also needed the water and the boats.

The iterations

I made eleven numbered proposals in three families: the crest over water, geometric designs with a wading bird and a ship's wheel, and a rounded cross device. The adopted design was proposal four, from the crest family.

Outcome

The community adopted the design and the flag now flies.
